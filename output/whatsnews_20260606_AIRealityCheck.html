Markets hit the brakes this week. With major indexes falling after setting record highs just last week, a strong jobs report and rising tensions in the Middle East stoked fears about inflation. A conservative revenue outlook from one of the world's biggest chip makers caused investors to question whether AI growth expectations were beginning to lose touch with reality and a sale by one of Bitcoin's biggest bulls triggered a crypto selloff. The tech heavy NASDAQ tumbled 4.7% and the S&P snapped a nine-week winning streak ending 2.6% lower. Those were the worst weekly declines for those indexes so far this year. The Dow declined 0.3%. 本周市场踩下了刹车。就在主要股指上周刚刚创下历史新高之后，一份强劲的就业报告以及中东地区不断升级的紧张局势重新点燃了市场对通胀的担忧。一家全球最大的芯片制造商给出了相对保守的营收展望，这让投资者开始怀疑，市场对于人工智能增长的预期是否已经开始脱离现实；与此同时，比特币最坚定的支持者之一减持持仓，也引发了加密货币市场的抛售潮。以科技股为主的纳斯达克指数暴跌4.7%，标普500指数结束了连续九周上涨的纪录，本周下跌2.6%。这是这两个指数今年以来表现最差的一周。道琼斯工业平均指数则下跌了0.3%。

The AI trade hit a speed bump this week as one of the industry's biggest chip makers prompted investors to rethink some of their loftiest expectations. Broadcom reported an explosive 48% surge in revenue and said it expects to generate more than $100 billion in annual AI semiconductor revenue by next year. Most people would think that those numbers would send a stock soaring. Instead, investors erased roughly $300 billion from Broadcom's market value in a single day. Now let's make that make sense. Analysts say the decline wasn't really about Broadcom's results. It was about its outlook. Investors have gotten used to AI chip companies exceeding expectations and raising their outlooks. NVIDIA has repeatedly blown past expectations with its quarterly numbers and earlier this week ST microelectronics nearly doubled its revenue guidance from above $500 million to well above one billion. But Broadcom held the line. Executives stuck with the company's previous forecast, suggesting growth remained strong but maybe not accelerating as quickly as investors had hoped. Broadcom shares ended the week more than 13% lower. The reaction rippled across the chip sector as investors reconsidered just how much future growth is already priced into some of the market's biggest winners. Shares of Micron dropped 11% and AMD fell roughly 10%. 本周，AI投资主题遭遇了一次减速带。行业内一家最大的芯片制造商让投资者开始重新审视他们最乐观的预期。博通（Broadcom）公布营收同比大增48%，并表示预计到明年其年度AI半导体业务收入将超过1000亿美元。大多数人会认为，这样的数据足以推动股价大幅上涨。然而事实恰恰相反，投资者在一天之内就抹去了博通大约3000亿美元的市值。那么这该如何解释呢？分析师表示，市场下跌其实并不是因为博通的业绩，而是因为它的未来展望。投资者已经习惯了AI芯片公司不断超出预期并持续上调业绩指引。英伟达（NVIDIA）多次公布远超预期的季度业绩，而就在本周稍早时候，意法半导体（STMicroelectronics）甚至将其收入指引从略高于5亿美元上调至超过10亿美元。但博通却没有这么做。管理层维持了此前的预测，这表明增长依然强劲，但增长速度可能并没有投资者期待的那么快。博通股价本周累计下跌超过13%。这一反应迅速蔓延至整个芯片板块，因为投资者开始重新评估，市场中这些最大赢家的股价究竟已经提前反映了多少未来增长。美光（Micron）股价下跌11%，AMD则下跌约10%。

The conflict in the Middle East came back into focus on Wall Street this week after fresh missile attacks raised questions about the future of the Strait of Hormuz, helping to make energy the best performing sector in the S&P 500. Brent Crude rose about 2% to $93.09 a barrel as another week passed by without a peace deal, reinforcing concerns that supply disruptions could last longer than expected. Until recently, investors had largely looked past the war, betting that ample oil inventories would help cushion the global economy from supply disruptions, but that assumption is starting to crack. Commodity trading giant, Trafigura, warned this week that energy markets are approaching an inflection point as inventories shrink and supply constraints persist. If a peace deal is reached soon, analysts say it could take months for oil production and shipping flows to fully recover. The S&P Energy Index rose about two and a half percent over the week. 本周，中东冲突再次成为华尔街关注的焦点。新一轮导弹袭击让市场开始担忧霍尔木兹海峡的未来局势，也推动能源板块成为标普500指数中表现最好的行业。由于和平协议迟迟未能达成，布伦特原油价格上涨约2%，达到每桶93.09美元，进一步强化了市场对于供应中断持续时间可能超出预期的担忧。直到不久前，投资者基本上还在忽视这场战争，他们认为充足的石油库存足以缓冲供应中断对全球经济带来的影响。但这种假设如今开始出现裂痕。大宗商品交易巨头托克集团（Trafigura）本周警告称，随着库存下降以及供应限制持续存在，能源市场正接近一个关键拐点。分析师表示，即使和平协议很快达成，石油产量和航运流量要恢复正常也可能需要数月时间。标普能源指数本周上涨约2.5%。

And even after this week's chip stock slump, the AI trade has helped push major US indexes to record highs. But there's one corner of the market missing out on the rally, crypto. Bitcoin has fallen 30% this year and lost about half of its values in speaking at above $126,000 last October. Analysts say one reason is that crypto is no longer the market's favorite speculative trade. For much of the past year, investors who once chased Bitcoin gains have instead been piling into AI stocks. While Bitcoin struggles, semiconductor stocks are dominating Wall Street's leaderboard for the year. One crypto trader put it bluntly. What Bitcoin needs is for some air to come out of the AI trade. The shift in investor retention is now beginning to pressure some of crypto's biggest believers. Strategy, one of Bitcoin's most outspoken supporters, sold a portion of its holdings for the first time since the last crypto winter in 2022. The moves rattled investors because strategy executive chairman Michael Saylor built a reputation on a simple message. Never sell your Bitcoin. Shares of crypto-linked companies ended the week lower. Strategy tumbled about 24%. Coinbase slipped 19% and Robinhood dropped more than 12%. 尽管本周芯片股遭遇重挫，但AI投资热潮仍然推动美国主要股指屡创新高。不过，市场中有一个板块却完全没能分享到这波上涨行情，那就是加密货币。比特币今年以来已经下跌30%，相比去年10月超过12.6万美元的高点，市值已经蒸发了大约一半。分析师表示，其中一个原因是，加密货币已经不再是市场最受欢迎的投机性交易。过去一年中，那些曾经追逐比特币收益的投资者，转而大量涌入AI概念股。比特币表现低迷的同时，半导体股票则牢牢占据着华尔街年度涨幅榜的前列。一位加密货币交易员直言不讳地表示，比特币真正需要的是AI交易热潮降温一些。投资者偏好的转移如今已经开始给加密货币最坚定的支持者带来压力。作为比特币最著名支持者之一的 Strategy 公司，自2022年上一轮加密货币寒冬以来首次出售了部分持仓。这一举动令投资者感到不安，因为 Strategy 执行董事长 Michael Saylor 一直以一句简单的话闻名——“永远不要卖掉你的比特币”。与加密货币相关的上市公司本周普遍下跌。Strategy 股价暴跌约24%，Coinbase 下跌19%，Robinhood 跌幅超过12%。
